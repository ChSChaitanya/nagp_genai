# Singapore Sample Itineraries and Things to Do

## Three-Day Classic Singapore Itinerary

### Day 1: Marina Bay and the City Centre

**Morning (9:00 AM - 12:00 PM)**
- Start at the Merlion Park for an iconic photo with the Singapore skyline.
- Walk to the Esplanade Theatres on the Bay and explore the free public art installations.
- Visit the National Gallery Singapore (allow 1.5-2 hours). Highlights include the DBS Singapore Gallery and the UOB Southeast Asia Gallery.

**Afternoon (12:00 PM - 5:00 PM)**
- Lunch at Lau Pa Sat hawker centre (try the satay street for grilled skewers).
- Visit Gardens by the Bay. Explore the Flower Dome and Cloud Forest conservatories (2-3 hours). The outdoor Supertree Grove is free.
- Walk across the Helix Bridge for views of the Marina Bay skyline.

**Evening (5:00 PM - 9:00 PM)**
- Take the lift to the Marina Bay Sands SkyPark Observation Deck for sunset views (SGD 26 admission).
- Dinner at one of the Marina Bay Sands restaurants or Satay by the Bay at Gardens by the Bay.
- Watch the Spectra light and water show at Marina Bay Sands (8:00 PM or 9:00 PM, free).
- Enjoy the Garden Rhapsody light show at Supertree Grove (7:45 PM and 8:45 PM, free).

### Day 2: Cultural Heritage Trail

**Morning (9:00 AM - 12:00 PM)**
- Start in Chinatown. Visit the Buddha Tooth Relic Temple (free entry, open 7 AM).
- Explore the Chinatown Heritage Centre to learn about early Chinese immigrants.
- Walk through the colourful Chinatown street market (Pagoda Street and Trengganu Street).

**Afternoon (12:00 PM - 5:00 PM)**
- Lunch at Maxwell Food Centre (try Tian Tian Hainanese Chicken Rice).
- Take the MRT to Little India. Visit the Sri Veeramakaliamman Temple.
- Explore Tekka Centre for Indian food and browse the Mustafa Centre.
- Walk to Kampong Glam. Visit the Sultan Mosque and stroll along Haji Lane.

**Evening (5:00 PM - 9:00 PM)**
- Tea or snacks at an Arab Street cafe.
- Dinner at a Kampong Glam restaurant (Turkish, Middle Eastern, or Malay cuisine).
- Evening walk along Bugis Street night market for bargain shopping.

### Day 3: Nature, Wildlife, and Sentosa

**Morning (9:00 AM - 12:00 PM)**
- Visit the Singapore Zoo or Bird Paradise (Mandai Wildlife Reserve). The zoo opens at 8:30 AM. Allow 3-4 hours for a full visit. Must-see: the Orangutan free-ranging exhibit and the Rainforest Kidzworld for families.

**Afternoon (12:00 PM - 6:00 PM)**
- Head to Sentosa Island. Take the Sentosa Express monorail from VivoCity (SGD 4).
- Options: Universal Studios Singapore (full day if preferred), S.E.A. Aquarium (2-3 hours), or beach time at Palawan Beach.
- Try the Skyline Luge (SGD 25 for 2 rides) for a fun downhill ride with skyride views.

**Evening (6:00 PM - 9:00 PM)**
- Dinner at VivoCity food court or the Sentosa waterfront restaurants.
- Watch the Wings of Time night show at Sentosa (7:40 PM or 8:40 PM, SGD 23).

## Five-Day Extended Itinerary

### Day 4: Neighbourhoods and Local Life

**Morning (9:00 AM - 12:00 PM)**
- Breakfast: Traditional kaya toast, soft-boiled eggs, and kopi at Ya Kun Kaya Toast or Chin Mee Chin Confectionery.
- Visit Tiong Bahru neighbourhood - Singapore's oldest housing estate turned hipster enclave. Explore independent bookstores, cafes, and the Tiong Bahru Market.

**Afternoon (12:00 PM - 5:00 PM)**
- Lunch at Tiong Bahru Market (try chwee kueh and lor mee).
- Visit the Singapore Botanic Gardens (free entry, UNESCO World Heritage Site). The National Orchid Garden within the Botanic Gardens requires a ticket (SGD 15) and features over 1,000 orchid species.
- Stroll along the Dempsey Hill area for art galleries and cafes.

**Evening (5:00 PM - 9:00 PM)**
- Visit the Orchard Road shopping belt. Stop at ION Orchard's ION Sky observation deck (free, level 56).
- Dinner at Newton Food Centre hawker centre (featured in the film Crazy Rich Asians).

### Day 5: East Coast, Changi, and Departure

**Morning (9:00 AM - 12:00 PM)**
- Cycle or walk along East Coast Park (bicycle rental available from SGD 8/hour).
- Brunch at one of the East Coast seafood restaurants or East Coast Lagoon Food Village.

**Afternoon (12:00 PM onward)**
- Visit Jewel Changi Airport. See the Rain Vortex waterfall, explore Canopy Park (SGD 7), shop at over 280 retail stores, and dine at one of 60+ eateries.
- If time permits, visit the Changi Chapel and Museum (free entry).

## Family-Friendly Itinerary (3 Days)

### Day 1: Wildlife and Nature
- **Singapore Zoo** (half day) - Interactive feeding sessions, splash zone at Rainforest Kidzworld.
- **River Wonders** (2-3 hours) - Amazon River Quest boat ride, giant panda exhibit.
- **Night Safari** (evening, 7:15 PM onwards) - Tram ride through nocturnal animal habitats, Creatures of the Night show.

### Day 2: Sentosa Island
- **Universal Studios Singapore** (full day) - Kid-friendly zones: Madagascar, Far Far Away, and Sesame Street (at suitable times). Height requirements vary by ride.
- Alternative: **S.E.A. Aquarium** (morning) + **Adventure Cove Waterpark** (afternoon) for water-loving families.
- **Palawan Beach** - The Southernmost Point of Continental Asia viewing platform, playground, and shallow wading pools.

### Day 3: Gardens and Museums
- **Gardens by the Bay** - Children's Garden (free, with water play area), Flower Dome, Cloud Forest.
- **ArtScience Museum** - Interactive Future World exhibit (permanent, family-friendly).
- **Marina Barrage** - Fly kites on the rooftop green space (free).
- Evening: **Singapore Flyer** for city views + dinner at a family-friendly restaurant.

## Cultural Immersion Itinerary (2 Days)

### Day 1: Heritage and History
- **Peranakan Museum** (reopened 2023) - Explore the unique Straits Chinese culture through exhibits on food, fashion, and rituals.
- **Katong and Joo Chiat** - Walk through the colourful Peranakan shophouses on Koon Seng Road. Try Katong laksa at 328 Katong Laksa.
- **Kampong Glam** - Deep dive into Malay heritage: Malay Heritage Centre, Gedung Kuning (Yellow Mansion), perfume shops on Arab Street.
- Evening: Attend a performance at the Esplanade or catch live music at one of the many bars in the area.

### Day 2: Food and Craft
- **Morning cooking class** - Learn to make Hainanese chicken rice, laksa, or Peranakan dishes. Several operators in Chinatown and Little India offer half-day classes.
- **Chinatown walking tour** - Explore the traditional medicine shops, tea houses, and calligraphy stores along Sago Street and Mosque Street.
- **Thian Hock Keng Temple** - One of Singapore's oldest and most important Hokkien temples (free entry).
- Afternoon: Visit the **Indian Heritage Centre** in Little India (SGD 8 admission).

## Indoor Attractions (for Rainy Days)

Singapore has many excellent indoor attractions, perfect for avoiding the rain or escaping the heat:

- **National Gallery Singapore** - Southeast Asia's largest art collection with over 8,000 artworks. Allow 2-3 hours.
- **ArtScience Museum** - Interactive exhibitions blending art and technology. The Future World permanent exhibit is especially engaging.
- **Gardens by the Bay Conservatories** - The Flower Dome and Cloud Forest are fully air-conditioned.
- **S.E.A. Aquarium** - One of the world's largest aquariums with over 100,000 marine animals.
- **National Museum of Singapore** - Singapore's oldest museum with immersive historical exhibits. (SGD 15 admission for non-residents.)
- **Science Centre Singapore** - Over 1,000 interactive exhibits, an IMAX theatre, and a snow city experience.
- **Jewel Changi Airport** - The Rain Vortex, Canopy Park, and extensive shopping and dining.
- **Universal Studios Singapore** - Many rides are indoor or covered.
- **Marina Bay Sands Shoppes** - Luxury shopping, the Sampan Ride canal boat, and the Digital Light Canvas.
- **Singapore Discovery Centre** - Interactive exhibits on Singapore's history, defence, and future.
- **Madame Tussauds Singapore** - Wax figures of celebrities and world leaders at Sentosa.
- **iFly Singapore** - Indoor skydiving experience at Sentosa (from SGD 89).
- **Kidzania Singapore** - Role-playing theme park for children at Sentosa.

## Outdoor Activities

### Nature Walks and Parks
- **MacRitchie Reservoir TreeTop Walk** - A 250-metre freestanding suspension bridge at 25 metres above the forest floor. The full loop trail is about 11 kilometres (3-4 hours).
- **Southern Ridges** - A 10-kilometre trail connecting Mount Faber Park, Telok Blangah Hill Park, HortPark, Kent Ridge Park, and Labrador Nature Reserve. The Henderson Waves bridge is a highlight.
- **Pulau Ubin** - A rustic island off Singapore's northeast coast, accessible by bumboat from Changi Point Ferry Terminal (SGD 4). Explore by bicycle, visit Chek Jawa Wetlands, and experience kampong (village) life.
- **Sungei Buloh Wetland Reserve** - A nature reserve in the northwest known for mangroves, migratory birds, and mudskippers. Best visited early morning.
- **Coney Island Park** - A 50-hectare island park in the northeast with beaches, mangroves, and casuarina woodlands. Accessible on foot or bicycle from Punggol.

### Water Activities
- **Kayaking at Pulau Ubin or Sentosa** - Guided kayak tours through mangroves and coastal waters.
- **Stand-up Paddleboarding** - Available at East Coast Park, Sentosa, and various reservoirs.
- **Cable Skiing at Singapore Wake Park** - Located at East Coast Park (from SGD 60/hour).

### Cycling
- **Park Connector Network (PCN)** - Over 300 kilometres of linked cycling and walking paths across the island.
- **East Coast Park** - A popular 15-kilometre coastal cycling route. Bicycle rentals are available at multiple points.
- **Pulau Ubin** - The best place for off-road mountain biking in Singapore.

## Nightlife and Evening Activities

- **Clarke Quay** - Singapore's nightlife hub along the river with bars, clubs, and restaurants. Popular venues include Zouk, Attica, and Level Up.
- **Marina Bay Sands** - CE LA VI rooftop bar for sunset cocktails with panoramic views.
- **1-Altitude** - The world's highest al-fresco bar at 282 metres (63rd floor of One Raffles Place).
- **Night Safari** - The world's first nocturnal wildlife park (7:15 PM - midnight).
- **Chinatown Night Market** - Browse souvenirs and enjoy street food along Pagoda Street.
- **Ann Siang Hill and Club Street** - Sophisticated cocktail bars and wine bars in restored shophouses.
- **Boat Quay** - Riverside bars and restaurants with views of the CBD skyline.

## Budget Tips

- Use the **Singapore Tourist Pass** for unlimited public transport.
- Eat at **hawker centres** for authentic and affordable meals.
- Visit **free attractions**: Merlion Park, Supertree Grove, Marina Barrage, Singapore Botanic Gardens, Esplanade.
- Book attraction tickets in advance through **Klook** or **KKday** for discounts of 10-30%.
- Visit museums on **free admission days** (check individual museum websites).
- The **Singapore Flyer** and **Gardens by the Bay** offer combo tickets at reduced rates.
- Stay in hostels in **Little India** or **Chinatown** (from SGD 25-40/night for dorms).
- Use the **Grab** app for competitive ride-hailing fares (often cheaper than taxis during non-peak hours).
